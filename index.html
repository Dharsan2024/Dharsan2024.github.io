What is Rush Hour?

RUSH HOUR is a 24-hours hackathon designed to promote creativity, innovation, and real-world problem solving. Participants collaborate, build impactful solutions, interact with industry experts, and push technological boundaries.

This high-energy event brings together the brightest minds from across India to solve challenges across multiple cutting-edge technology domains.
